SPIDER-VERSE VILLAINS

The Most Dangerous Foes in Spider-Man's Rogues Gallery

Meet the Enemies

From brilliant masterminds to unstoppable powerhouses, Spider-Man faces some of the most iconic villains in comic book history. Each brings unique threats, complex motivations, and challenges that push the wall-crawler to his limits.

Arch-Nemesis

Green Goblin

Norman Osborn, a brilliant industrialist driven mad by experimental serum, becomes Spider-Man's most personal and dangerous enemy. Armed with pumpkin bombs, glider, and cunning intellect, the Green Goblin represents chaos and madness incarnate.

Learn More →
Genius

Doctor Octopus

Dr. Otto Octavius, brilliant scientist with four mechanical tentacles fused to his body, combines genius intellect with devastating physical power. His superiority complex and obsession with proving himself make him one of Spider-Man's most persistent foes.

Learn More →
Symbiote

Venom

Eddie Brock bonded with an alien symbiote rejected by Spider-Man, creating a dark mirror of the hero. With all of Spider-Man's powers plus invisibility and immunity to spider-sense, Venom is both terrifying predator and occasional lethal protector.

Learn More →
Shape-Shifter

Sandman

Flint Marko, transformed into living sand after nuclear accident, can reshape his body at will and become virtually indestructible. A sympathetic villain driven by desperation to provide for his daughter, making him complex and tragic.

Learn More →
Transformation

Lizard

Dr. Curt Connors, whose attempt to regrow his lost arm transformed him into reptilian monster. The internal battle between good man and savage beast creates one of Spider-Man's most psychologically complex and tragic villains.

Learn More →
Living Lightning

Electro

Max Dillon, electrical lineman who gained ability to generate and control massive amounts of electricity. A bitter nobody given ultimate power, proving that abilities amplify character flaws rather than fix them.

Learn More →
Master of Illusion

Mysterio

Quentin Beck, Hollywood special effects artist who weaponized illusions and psychology to become one of Spider-Man's most dangerous foes. In an age of deepfakes and misinformation, his manipulation of perception feels eerily relevant.

Learn More →
Aerial Predator

Vulture

Adrian Toomes, elderly inventor whose electromagnetic harness grants flight and enhanced vitality. Betrayed by his business partner, this bitter genius proves that age and cunning can be as dangerous as youth and power.

Learn More →
Crime Lord

Kingpin

Wilson Fisk, the ultimate crime lord who controls New York's underworld through combination of peak human strength, genius intellect, and vast criminal empire. He represents systemic corruption that can't be defeated through simple punching.

Learn More →
Unstoppable Force

Rhino

Aleksei Sytsevich, Russian thug transformed into living weapon with suit bonded permanently to his body. A working-class tragedy of exploitation, representing desperate people accepting terrible bargains from those in power.
