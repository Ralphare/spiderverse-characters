Spider-Man Versions

From the classic Peter Parker to heroes across the multiverse, discover the many faces behind the mask. Each Spider-Person brings unique abilities, backgrounds, and perspectives to the heroic legacy.

Original

Peter Parker (Earth-616)

The original Spider-Man from the main Marvel Universe. After being bitten by a radioactive spider, teenager Peter Parker gained incredible powers and learned that with great power comes great responsibility.

Raimi Films

Tobey Maguire Spider-Man

From Sam Raimi's beloved trilogy (2002-2007), this Peter Parker features organic web-shooters and iconic battles against Green Goblin, Doc Ock, and Venom. A defining portrayal of the character for a generation.

Learn More →
Amazing

Andrew Garfield Spider-Man

From The Amazing Spider-Man films (2012-2014), featuring a more quippy, skateboarding Peter with mechanical web-shooters. His emotional journey with Gwen Stacy remains one of cinema's most heartbreaking superhero stories.

Learn More →
MCU

Tom Holland Spider-Man

The MCU's Spider-Man (2016-present), mentored by Tony Stark and fighting alongside the Avengers. Youngest and most tech-enhanced version, with Stark tech suit and "Peter Tingle" replacing traditional spider-sense.

Learn More →
Ultimate

Miles Morales

Brooklyn teenager who gained spider powers in the Ultimate Universe. With unique abilities like invisibility and venom strike, Miles proves anyone can wear the mask and became the first Afro-Latino Spider-Man.

Learn More →
Future

Spider-Man 2099

Miguel O'Hara from the year 2099, with enhanced vision, organic webbing, talons, and venomous fangs. A geneticist who gained powers through futuristic experiments, protecting Nueva York from corporate tyranny.

Learn More →
Earth-65

Spider-Gwen

Gwen Stacy from Earth-65, where she was bitten by the spider instead of Peter Parker. A drummer in a band and hero driven by the tragedy of her universe's Peter, combining style with substance.

Learn More →
1930s

Spider-Man Noir

From a dark 1930s universe, this Peter Parker operates as a detective-style hero fighting crime in Depression-era New York. With mystical spider powers and trench coat aesthetic, he's noir cinema personified.

Learn More →
Cartoon

Spider-Ham

Peter Porker from a cartoon universe where a spider was bitten by a radioactive pig. Operating on cartoon physics with hammerspace and fourth-wall awareness, he's comedic chaos personified as a superhero.

Learn More →
Mumbattan

Spider-Man India

Pavitr Prabhakar from Mumbai, gaining powers through mystical means rather than science. Fighting to protect Mumbattan with optimism and cultural pride, representing Spider-Man's universal appeal across cultures.

Spider-Man Villains

From criminal masterminds to tragic monsters, Spider-Man faces one of comics' greatest rogues galleries. Each villain brings unique challenges, complex motivations, and unforgettable stories.

Arch-Nemesis

Green Goblin

Norman Osborn, driven mad by experimental serum, becomes Spider-Man's most personal and dangerous enemy. Armed with pumpkin bombs and cunning intellect, he represents chaos and knows Peter's secret identity.

Learn More →
Genius

Doctor Octopus

Dr. Otto Octavius with four mechanical tentacles fused to his body, combining genius intellect with devastating physical power. His superiority complex makes him one of Spider-Man's most persistent foes.

Learn More →
Symbiote

Venom

Eddie Brock bonded with alien symbiote rejected by Spider-Man, creating a dark mirror of the hero. With all Spider-Man's powers plus more, Venom is both terrifying predator and occasional protector.

Learn More →
Shape-Shifter

Sandman

Flint Marko transformed into living sand, able to reshape his body and become virtually indestructible. A sympathetic villain driven by desperation to provide for his daughter.

Learn More →
Transformation

Lizard

Dr. Curt Connors' attempt to regrow his lost arm transformed him into reptilian monster. The battle between good man and savage beast creates one of Spider-Man's most tragic villains.

Learn More →
Living Lightning

Electro

Max Dillon gained ability to generate and control massive electricity. A bitter nobody given ultimate power, proving abilities amplify character flaws rather than fix them.

Learn More →
Master of Illusion

Mysterio

Quentin Beck weaponized Hollywood special effects and illusions to become one of Spider-Man's most dangerous psychological foes. In the age of deepfakes, his manipulation feels eerily relevant.

Learn More →
Aerial Predator

Vulture

Adrian Toomes' electromagnetic harness grants flight and enhanced vitality. Betrayed by his business partner, this elderly genius proves age and cunning can be as dangerous as youth and power.

Learn More →
Crime Lord

Kingpin

Wilson Fisk controls New York's underworld through peak human strength, genius intellect, and vast criminal empire. He represents systemic corruption that can't be defeated through punching.

Learn More →
Unstoppable Force

Rhino

Aleksei Sytsevich transformed into living weapon with indestructible suit bonded to his body. A working-class tragedy representing desperate people accepting terrible bargains from those in power.

Learn More →
